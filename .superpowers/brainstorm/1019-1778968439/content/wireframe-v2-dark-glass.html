Design Notes

Background: Full black (#000) — bukan #0D1B3D. Primary dark hanya untuk aksen.
Aksen: Cyan #00FFFF — CTA, highlights, logo, glow effects. Branding Pacman tetap.
Liquid Glass: Blur + gradient border pseudo-element. Digunakan di navbar, cards, buttons.
Hero BG: Animated gradient + floating particles (bukan video). Subtle cyan glow.
Font: Instrument Serif (headings) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body/UI). Dual font system.
Multi-page: React Router — Home, /pricing, /portfolio. Navbar links ke masing-masing page.
